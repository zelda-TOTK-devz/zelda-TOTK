## 🧭 Pro Tips for Tears of the Kingdom

Getting the most out of this **The Legend of Zelda Tears of the Kingdom NSP** takes more than just running in a straight line. Follow these tips from experienced players:

1. **Unlock the Skyview Towers first** — Each tower reveals a huge chunk of the map and launches you into the sky, which is the fastest way to travel early on
2. **Light up the Depths early** — Every Lightroot you activate lights up a large area of the Depths and makes it far less scary to explore
3. **Fuse everything** — A plain stick fused with a rock out-damages most early weapons. Fuse arrows with Keese eyeballs for easy headshots
4. **Cook with purpose** — Hearty meals give bonus hearts, and Sundelion dishes cure gloom damage. Always carry a few of each
5. **Save your Zonaite** — Zonaite powers your Zonai devices and upgrades your Energy Cell. Mine it in the Depths and spend it wisely
6. **Talk to everyone** — Side quests in this game hide some of the best rewards, including armor sets, new abilities and entire quest chains
7. **Use Recall on everything** — Falling rocks, thrown weapons, even enemy attacks can be reversed. Recall is the most underrated ability in the game

  **Solution:** Lower the resolution scale to 0.75x, enable multicore CPU settings, and close background apps. A 60 FPS mod also helps on strong PCs.

- ❌ **Save File Not Loading**
  Saves from a different region or version can conflict.

- ❌ **Update NSP Fails to Install**
